Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Rock Simulator, a mobile game developed and published by RhythymGames.

1. Information We Collect

RhythymGames does not directly collect or store personal information from users through Rock Simulator.

The game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, telephone number, home address, or other identifying information in order to play the game.

2. Advertising

Rock Simulator uses Unity Ads to display advertisements.

Unity Ads is a third-party advertising service. When advertisements are displayed, Unity and its service providers may automatically process certain technical and advertising-related information from the device.

Depending on the device, operating system, user settings, and applicable laws, this information may include information such as:

  • Device and hardware information
  • Operating system information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address or network-related information
  • Information about advertisements displayed or interacted with
  • Technical information required to provide advertising services

Unity processes information according to its own privacy practices and policies.

3. Third-Party Services

Rock Simulator uses third-party services that are necessary for certain application functionality.

The primary third-party service used by the game for advertising is Unity Ads.

Third-party service providers may collect and process technical information according to their own privacy policies. RhythymGames does not control how third-party providers process information.

4. Game Purchase

Rock Simulator is a paid application available through Google Play.

The purchase of Rock Simulator is processed through Google Play. RhythymGames does not directly collect or store users' payment card, bank account, or other payment credentials.

5. Game Progress and Local Data

Rock Simulator may temporarily use information stored locally on the device while the game is running, such as current gameplay state or temporary progress.

Rock Simulator does not provide user accounts or cloud-based player profiles. Gameplay progress is not intended to be permanently associated with a user account and may be reset when the application is closed.

6. Internet Connection

Rock Simulator requires an internet connection for certain functionality, including services such as advertising.

When the application connects to the internet, network and technical information may be processed by the operating system, Google Play, Unity Ads, or other services required for the application to operate.

7. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Rock Simulator does not intentionally collect personal information through an account or user-registration system.

At the time this Privacy Policy was prepared, RhythymGames had not identified a separate analytics or crash-reporting service that intentionally collects personal information directly from users.

Third-party services integrated into the application may process technical information necessary for their own services. Such processing is governed by the respective third party's policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Rock Simulator is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

The game does not require users to provide personal information to create an account or play the game.

Because the game uses third-party advertising services, parents and guardians should review the privacy practices of those services and the applicable settings provided by the device and Google Play.

9. Data Security

RhythymGames takes reasonable measures to protect the application and information handled through it.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

RhythymGames does not maintain user accounts or a database containing users' personal information through Rock Simulator.

As a result, there is no personal user account data maintained by RhythymGames that users need to request deletion of.

Information processed by third-party services, including advertising services, is subject to the respective provider's retention and deletion policies.

11. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Third-party services used by Rock Simulator may have their own privacy policies explaining how information is collected, used, shared, retained, and deleted.

Users should review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party service providers for additional information about their data practices.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

RhythymGames may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this webpage and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Rock Simulator, please contact us.
